How to Play

  1. Start: Pick Human or Computer for each colour and press Play.
  2. Dice: Tap the dice or the Roll button to get 1 to 6.
  3. Leaving home: A token leaves base only on a 6.
  4. Moving: Tap a glowing token to move it exactly the dice value.
  5. Capturing: Landing on a rival token (outside safe cells) sends it home and gives you another turn.
  6. Safe cells: Star cells and coloured start cells protect tokens.
  7. Rolling 6: You roll again. Three sixes in a row cancel your turn.
  8. Reaching home: A token needs the exact number to enter the centre. It gives you another turn.
  9. Winning: First player to bring all 4 tokens home wins.
